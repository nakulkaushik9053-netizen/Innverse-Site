* **Eyebrow:** `Grade-Wise Progression`

* **Subtext:** `Designed in accordance with NEP-2020 and CBSE Skill Subject mandates. Students advance from visual algorithmic logic to autonomous robotics and AI.`

  1. **Grades 3 - 5: Curiosity & Logical Foundations (Level 1: Foundations)**
     - *Theme:* Observe & Play | *Hours:* 32 Hours / Year
     - *Topics:* Block-based Scratch coding, Simple machines, Paper circuits & safe LED loops, Algorithmic patterns.
     - *Tools:* Scratch 3.0, Craft Electronics, Logic Puzzles, Tinkercad Shapes.
     - *Capstone:* Interactive Animated Storybook & Musical Greeting Card.
     - *Skills:* Computational Thinking, Fine Motor Skills, Creative Expression.
  2. **Grades 6 - 8: Hands-on Microcontrollers & Sensors (Level 2: Builders - Popular)**
     - *Theme:* Build & Measure | *Hours:* 48 Hours / Year
     - *Topics:* Arduino hardware architecture & pinouts, Sensor interfacing (LDR, Ultrasonic, DHT11), Actuators (Servos, Motors, Relays), C++ syntax, CircuitLab simulation.
     - *Tools:* CircuitLab Simulator, Arduino UNO, Breadboards & Sensors, Tinkercad 3D.
     - *Capstone:* Autonomous Obstacle-Avoiding Rover & Smart Touchless Dustbin.
     - *Skills:* Hardware Debugging, C++ Coding, Spatial 3D Modeling.
  3. **Grades 9 - 10: IoT, Automation & Applied Robotics (Level 3: Innovators)**
     - *Theme:* Connect & Automate | *Hours:* 54 Hours / Year
     - *Topics:* ESP32 Wi-Fi / Bluetooth IoT systems, Cloud dashboards (Blynk, Adafruit IO), Dual motor driver kinematics, Python scripting, Rapid 3D CAD slicing.
     - *Tools:* ESP32 IoT Kits, Python 3, 3D Printers & Slicers, Cloud IoT Brokers.
     - *Capstone:* IoT Smart Greenhouse with Mobile Telemetry & Line Following Bot.
     - *Skills:* Cloud Architecture, Python Programming, Kinematics & Mechanics.
  4. **Grades 11 - 12: Artificial Intelligence, Drones & Capstones (Level 4: Future Engineers)**
     - *Theme:* Predict & Deploy | *Hours:* 60 Hours / Year
     - *Topics:* Edge AI Computer Vision (ESP32-CAM & OpenCV), Neural networks for object/gesture recognition, Drone aerodynamics & quadcopter flight, Custom PCB design, Patent filing.
     - *Tools:* ESP32-CAM, OpenCV / TensorFlow Lite, Drone Kits, EasyEDA PCB.
     - *Capstone:* AI Autonomous Camera Surveillance Turret & Quadcopter Flight Rig.
     - *Skills:* Computer Vision, Flight Aerodynamics, Research & Prototyping.
